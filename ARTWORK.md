# Entrance artwork

The recovery expansion adds `public/recovery-campus.svg`, original isometric geometry generated by `generate_recovery.py`. Its three highlighted operations represent recycling, waste collection/transfer, and compost/mulch processing. `generate_atlas_extensions.py` also adds the Recovery works satellite. These illustrations are conceptual, without surveyed dimensions or equipment capacities.

The family expansion preserves this entrance image. Its new neighborhood, school-campus, discovery-park, and satellite-district illustrations are original SVG geometry generated by `generate_community.py` and `generate_atlas_extensions.py`, using the project's existing `generate_atlas.py` drawing primitives. They are conceptual arrangements, not scaled site or equipment designs.

Method: built-in image-generation tool.

Project asset: `public/tiger-earth.png`.
