# Recovery campus and material pathways

The founder requested recycling, waste management, and a compost/mulch facility within the revised $150 million annual upkeep plan. The latest clarification sets the cost divisor to 10,000 residents, giving a full-budget average of $1,250 per person per month; household estimates allocate school separately. `#/recovery` supplies a physical campus concept, seven selectable material loops, and readiness explanations. It is reachable from the costs overview, the twelfth atlas district marker (Recovery works), and the existing recovery operating domain. The directory remains 24 operating domains.

## Proposed operations

- Recycling plant: intake, separation, storage, material processing, output qualification, and rejects.
- Waste management: collection, weighing, transfer, appropriate separation of streams, and accountable residual destinations.
- Compost/mulch facility: a managed food-scrap composting line and a separate clean woody-material mulch stream.

The original code-generated illustration is `public/recovery-campus.svg`; regenerate it with `python generate_recovery.py`. The atlas satellite is maintained in `generate_atlas_extensions.py`. These are conceptual layouts without selected acreage, construction dimensions, equipment sizing, or commissioned capacity.

These references support general process descriptions, not claims about Saber facilities. Aluminum remains in the metals loop. Plastic-derived fuel would require a distinct project and is not labeled biodiesel. Food scraps undergo managed composting rather than being presented as ready-to-use mulch. Plastic bottles follow their resin stream; glass bottles follow glass recovery.

No processing yields, recycling revenue, fuel-independence figures, individual facility budgets, or 100% recovery claims are invented. Every proposed operation needs feedstock characterization, qualified people and equipment, processing inputs, accepted output specifications, measured losses, and actual residual destinations. Internal capacity and any interim external processing must remain explicit. The added facilities are included within the unitemized $129 million operating remainder, rather than charged again on top of the total.
